DH116 single-finger fixture — actual Isaac results,2026-10-24

Scope and method
User authorized the proposed fixed-fixture loading test. Four separate hands test left/right thumb/index distal pads at the current grasp posture. The palm stays fixed; source hand mass/linear rigid mimic is retained, gravity disabled. Only the selected distal pad collision remains enabled. A kinematic frictionless plate is placed .5mm beyond the pad collider support surface along the grasp's world-X closing direction. No table, straw, PPO or real robot is involved. This fixture is not the manufacturer's unspecified force-test setup.

Drive gains20 Nm/rad,damping.2; target flexion ramps .05rad/s up to+.15rad,holds2s and unloads. The last1s is qualified from every480Hz physics sample, rejecting missing samples and any force below14N,>30N,mimic error>.02rad or >10%force variability. The entire trial is invalidated on any hard fault. Trace files contain60Hz joint states/actual command error/contact position and PD-estimated torque; hold480hz files retain all481 qualification samples. Torques are not solver-measured motor torques. Faulted trials are stopped/unloaded; their subsequent zero load is not rated capacity.

Interpretation
The current estimated simulator at this posture has a demonstrated loaded-coupling/steady-load-transfer limitation. Raising numerical iterations changes the threshold, so these numbers cannot be interpreted as actual DH116 hardware maximum force. Sleep-threshold comparison alone did not explain the steady-load loss. Exact root cause of the low steady force requires further drive/constraint-force diagnostics; do not label it fully diagnosed or resolved. Do not deploy2Nm merely because it was a test cap, and do not equate the14–16N manufacturer reference with a joint-torque parameter or the current two-pad load sum.
