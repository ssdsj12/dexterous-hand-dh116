# Fixed-pregrasp native settlement entry

The three published CPU pregrasp candidates now have a separate opt-in native settlement entry. The entry recomputes the exact CPU Reset seed and binds it to the published candidate SHA and the named training wire specification. Resizing places the wire and its two physical supports. Both measured hand seeds keep their authored poses and coupled joint positions. Pinch, Tripod and Power produce their own Reset, exchange and support evidence.

The native path reuses the original App ownership, two SDK setup steps, commanded-state and PhysX readback comparison, the source and locked-cooked hull checks, and the stationary-first-exchange tolerance. The one shared rod is read from its original runtime at Reset and from the shared support and palm runtime at the terminal physical tick. The quiet interval requires no registered hand contact and 48 consecutive same-clock measurements with both support loads. A dedicated plan version separates this settlement from the legacy Tripod approach handoff.

Run `scripts/python.sh -B -m scripts.verify_dh116_wire_variant_isaac --fixed-pregrasp --quiet-settlement-sdk 1426 ...` to freeze a recipe against an exact candidate and training catalog. Use `--verify-existing-plan` to check the frozen inputs. Each pending recipe has one environment on GPU 2, 1426 total SDK steps including two setup steps, 17088 solver substeps, zero control steps, zero PPO updates and a 600-second soft wall bound. The existing outer supervisor records the actual natural exit and hashes the run evidence. The three pending runs are separate and serial.

CPU verification covered 149 tests and froze Pinch, Tripod and Power on the same named 9 mm training specimen. The legacy Tripod recipe retained its seed, specimen, variant options and budget. A different candidate with a new file SHA and a held-out specimen both failed freeze verification. No Isaac process was started in this package. Native Reset, settlement, contact, Lift and Teacher promotion remain pending.
